# Change: 2026-09-25-co-creation-and-flow-integrity

## Why

The optimization plan (`docs/spec-prototype-co-creation-optimization-plan.md`) audited the spec-prototype skill at basis `74e2554` and confirmed two blocking defect classes: (1) stage and dialectic rules override the existing co-creation governance with fixed physical-metaphor-first rounds, mandatory craft defaults, and legacy core-workflow references that no longer own their behavior; (2) the canonical formal dispatch chain is broken at real seams — execution_boundary validates digest keys against retired legacy paths, the envelope injects six hardcoded global methods and a fixed 390px responsive rule regardless of Spec authorization, and PNG-only evidence freezes fail downstream admission because `retained()` cannot read directories. These defects make "what was discussed" diverge from "what gets built" and block canonical-only prototypes from dispatching at all.

## What Changes

- Wave 1 (rules/templates): converge active entry, lifecycle, and coverage ownership onto SKILL.md, core-kernel.md, and artifact-lifecycle.md; retire active references to core-workflow.md; unfix Stage 0/1 and the four dialectic rounds (no lockstep, no mandatory metaphor/material defaults, minimal-decision reopen); discussion template records actual user decision source.
- Wave 2 (seams): bind dispatch digest validation to the real envelope-recorded relative paths (canonical realpath, no symlink escape); strip the hardcoded method injector and fixed responsive_folding from builder_guidance in favor of Spec-authored declarations; make evidence-freeze bundle hashing recursive over directories and align downstream-gate status parsing.

## Non-Goals

- Restoring core-workflow.md as an authority; deleting it or editing archived Changes/baselines.
- Adding a Stage 1.5, a second co-creation flow, a style-pack entry, or a hidden execution DAG.
- Replacing the Nine Pillars or Five Axes; introducing fixed style lookup tables for craft axes.
- Progressive Schema tiers, tokens uni-directional derivation (Wave 3), Builder/Critic agent prompt and eval changes (Wave 4) — deferred to a successor Change after these two waves land.
- Modifying the Loom Runtime, generated artifacts, or historical reports.

## Impact

Rules and templates (8 markdown files), two dispatch scripts (`execution_boundary.py`, `assemble_envelope.py`), one freeze script (`handoff.py`), and their owner tests. Behavior after landing: canonical-only prototypes dispatch without legacy files; Builder receives only Spec-authorized methods; PNG-only evidence survives freeze and admission; feedback reopens only the affected design decisions. Owner tests updated in the same tasks; no product prototype artifacts are touched.
