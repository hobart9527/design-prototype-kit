## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: ENG-001 Canonical Five-Stage Lifecycle
The design engine SHALL select its working route from the requested outcome: exploration, new surface, local refinement, or formal handoff. Sense-making, modeling, prototyping, evaluation, and packaging SHALL remain available methods rather than mandatory sequential gates for every request. Exploration SHALL permit revisable source-linked briefs without frozen six-pillar contracts. New surfaces SHALL inherit established assets; local refinements SHALL preserve unrelated decisions. Formal handoff SHALL retain confirmed decisions, coverage, current evidence, limitations, and approval without treating file completeness or hashes as design quality.

#### Scenario: ENG-SCN-001 Five-stage sequential progression
- **WHEN** an exploration, new-surface, local-refinement, or formal-handoff request is handled
- **THEN** the entry instructions and owning workflow define the matching input, activity, stopping condition, and evidence requirements without forcing all requests through complete frozen contracts

### Requirement: ENG-002 Kinetic and Physical World Micro-interactions
The design engine SHALL derive interaction and expression from project decisions, task needs, and accessibility constraints rather than universal animation, palette, radius, font, density, or scaffold prescriptions. It SHALL preserve keyboard accessibility, usable target sizing, focus recovery, and reduced-motion support where applicable. Reference patterns SHALL be optional design aids, not mandatory page chassis.

#### Scenario: ENG-SCN-002 Natural easing and touch ergonomics adherence
- **WHEN** the Builder receives a reading product, mobile task flow, dense workbench, or refinement task
- **THEN** its instructions retain the project's expression and interaction authority without requiring AppState identifiers, global Space/P shortcuts, scale(0.97), industrial palettes, or scaffold slot filling

### Requirement: ENG-003 Five Essential Experience States
The design engine SHALL consider Loading, Empty, Partial, Error, and Overflow against the contracted task and exercise each applicable state plus consequential actions, cancellation, and recovery. Non-applicable states SHALL have an explicit scope reason, and untested required states SHALL remain unverified. A bounded Builder feedback loop SHALL allow targeted reading, implementation, runtime checks, screenshot inspection, repair, and re-verification. Resource exhaustion SHALL preserve unresolved work rather than imply completion.

#### Scenario: ENG-SCN-003 Complete experience state coverage
- **WHEN** a prototype needs visual or behavioral revision or the execution budget expires
- **THEN** Builder instructions permit further evidence-led repair or return unresolved scope and evidence limits, without a mandatory single-pass generation rule or universal eight-tool-turn ceiling
