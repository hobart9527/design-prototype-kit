## 1. Entry Check
- This topic is selectable, not sequenced: enter it when Materiality or Energy
  is the active uncertainty, regardless of which other topics have settled.
- Ground material substrate and kinetic response in whatever decisions the
  product has actually settled; a physical metaphor is an optional anchor.

  - *Base*: `#101418` (deep cold rock), hairline borders (`rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`).

## 3. Domain Color Discipline & Negative Declarations (9-Pillars: Color Semantics)
A Signature Accent is optional and decided per product, never a template mandate:
- **Signature Hue**: e.g., Authentic Cinnabar (`--accent-seal: #B3352B`) when the
  product's semantics genuinely call for an authority accent.
- **Negative boundaries are product-semantic rules**: any restriction (e.g. which
  states or actions may never carry the accent, and where the accent strikes) is
  authored from this product's own settled decisions and recorded with its
  product-semantic source — it is not inherited as a global template rule.

## 4. Kinetic Timing & Decisive Exchange 3-Frame (5-Axis: Energy)
Specify the physical response of the decisive commit action:
- *Timing*: Fixed duration authored from the product's energy decision (e.g. a
  mechanical press, `cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1)`); no template-fixed value.
- *Frame 1 (Intent)*: Pointer/key triggers tactile depression (`:active scale(0.98)`).
- *Frame 2 (Commit)*: Immediate state freeze; prevents double submission.
- *Frame 3 (Settlement)*: Badge transitions deterministically; focus deterministically restores to originating trigger.

## 5. Orthogonal 4-Axis Craft Stack (Authorable Axes)
When entered, the four orthogonal craft axes compile into `foundation.craft_stack`
(and project into `visual_directives.craft_stack`). They carry NO fixed method
defaults here: each axis value is authored from this product's own settled
decisions and records its product-semantic source. The 5-Dial register's explicit
`surface_optics: ...`-style declarations are the authoring route.

## 6. Settlement
When entered, lock the authored substrate tokens, `--accent-seal` (if a signature
accent was chosen), craft stack axes, and kinetic timing. This topic's settlement
does not gate any other topic.
