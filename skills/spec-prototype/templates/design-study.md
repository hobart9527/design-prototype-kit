# Optional prompts for a design study

Use inside the existing Discussion, comparison or Foundation when one uncertainty
needs development. This is not another required artifact or approval gate.

## Question and basis

- Product thesis and unresolved design question:
- Source, object/content, journey and Surface Topology references:
- Actor, task, use context and consequence:
- Evidence, competing interpretations and missing knowledge:
- Decision owner and stopping condition:

## Design Proposition

### Human-readable proposition synopsis

- Qualitative Design DNA (Axis DNA) — combination and product basis:
- Real-World Mapping status: `used | not needed | unverified`
- Signature Craft — concrete expression of the Signature Relationship in this
  specimen:

The synopsis aids comparison; the full causal proposition below remains the
design rationale. Axis DNA is qualitative and contextual, not a fixed score.

### Causal proposition

- Generative mechanism or lens and why it transfers:
- Same task-and-content specimen, viewport and state:
- Familiar convention deliberately retained:
- Cross-surface Signature Relationship:
- Integrated expression: content voice, hierarchy, typography, color, imagery,
  icons, component character, feedback and motion as applicable:
- Expected benefit:
- Cost and learning burden:
- Falsification condition and contrasting transfer case:
- Unsupported assumptions and implementation freedoms:

### Real-World Mapping (only when useful)

| Reference and source | Evidence status | Observed mechanism | Local translation | Non-transfer boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | `explicit | observed | derived | hypothesis | unknown` | | | |

Leave the table empty and explain `not needed` above when original product
reasoning is stronger than analogy. A reference contributes a local mechanism,
not its brand style, objects, permissions or workflow wholesale.

## Study and result

- Alternatives examined and variables held constant:
- Static, playable or Walking Skeleton fidelity chosen for this question:
- Rendered/experienced evidence and limitations:
- Strongest relationship to preserve:
- Professional critique and owning revision:
- Result: `supported | rejected | inconclusive`
- Upstream assumption reopened, or unaffected decisions preserved:
- Next action and required authority/evidence:

Resolve only the current uncertainty. Do not silently freeze page count or
interaction from a visual study, and do not ask the user to fill the template.
Use actual-size evidence where appearance matters and a real trace where behavior
matters. For mobile typography, include actual font/script and line/scan evidence
on a constrained and enlarged specimen.
