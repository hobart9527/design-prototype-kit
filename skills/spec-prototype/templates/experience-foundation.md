> Project-level integrated design baseline. Product sources own product facts;
> the retained Surface Map owns Surface Topology and journeys. Foundation owns the
> approved Design Proposition, expression system, adaptation boundaries and
> cross-surface invariants. It references rather than duplicates those owners.

### Human-readable proposition synopsis

- Qualitative Design DNA (Axis DNA) — combination and product basis:
  (name the axis values and state why each fits this product's actual user
